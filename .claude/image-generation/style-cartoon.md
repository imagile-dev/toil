Style: lighthearted editorial cartoon spot illustration, like a witty magazine or tech-blog illustration. Flat colour fills with bold, clean, even-weight ivory outlines, simple rounded shapes, expressive but minimal faces, a little visual joke that reads at a glance. Calm and professional in finish: no clutter, no gradients except the accent, no glossy 3D, no photorealism, no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos or brand marks.

Recurring character: "the crate", a small square cardboard package box with dot eyes, a small determined smile, stubby arms and legs, and a striped hard hat in a lighter blue. It is the hero of every scene and always competent, never the butt of the joke; the joke is on the bureaucracy around it.

Palette (use only these):
- Sky blue #6cb4ff (rgb 108,180,255) shading to pale blue #c2e0ff (rgb 194,224,255) for the crate and the key object
- Pale blue #c2e0ff on its own for small accents (the hard-hat stripe, a highlight). The palette is one hue: no orange, amber, red, green or purple anywhere
- Cool white #e8eaed (rgb 232,234,237) for outlines, paper and highlights
- Cool greys #a7afba and #4a525e for secondary characters and props
- Background: one solid flat #0a0c0f (rgb 10,12,15) with no texture, vignette, floor shadow or frame

Composition: one clear scene, generous empty space, nothing touching the edges, readable as a thumbnail.
