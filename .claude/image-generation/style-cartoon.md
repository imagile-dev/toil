Style: lighthearted editorial cartoon spot illustration, like a witty magazine or tech-blog illustration. Flat colour fills with bold, clean, even-weight ivory outlines, simple rounded shapes, expressive but minimal faces, a little visual joke that reads at a glance. Calm and professional in finish: no clutter, no gradients except the accent, no glossy 3D, no photorealism, no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos or brand marks.

Recurring character: "the crate", a small square cardboard package box with dot eyes, a small determined smile, stubby arms and legs, and a teal-striped hard hat. It is the hero of every scene and always competent, never the butt of the joke; the joke is on the bureaucracy around it.

- Ivory #f6f1e9 (rgb 246,241,233) for outlines, paper and highlights
- Teal #34e2c4 (rgb 52,226,196) for one or two small accents
- Warm greys #b4a998 and #5c5248 for secondary characters and props
- Background: one solid flat #16130f (rgb 22,19,15) with no texture, vignette, floor shadow or frame

Composition: one clear scene, generous empty space, nothing touching the edges, readable as a thumbnail.
