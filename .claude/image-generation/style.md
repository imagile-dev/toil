Style: precise technical line drawing, like an engineer's drafting sheet or a patent illustration, rendered as fine luminous linework. Isometric or orthographic projection, crisp single-weight strokes, small dimension ticks, registration marks and construction lines as subtle detail. Calm, exact, premium; never cartoonish, never glossy 3D, no gradients-as-decoration, no lens flare, no people, no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos.

Palette (use only these):
- Ivory linework #ece7df (rgb 236,231,223) for most strokes
- Flame orange #ff6a3d (rgb 255,106,61) for the focal element, shading into amber #ffb84d (rgb 255,184,77)
- Amber #ffb84d on its own for one or two small accents. All warm: no green, teal, blue or purple
- Muted taupe #a89e91 (rgb 168,158,145) for construction lines and secondary detail
- Background: fully transparent, or warm near-black #16130f (rgb 22,19,15) when transparency is impossible

Composition: generous negative space, subject centered or weighted right, nothing touching the edges, readable at small sizes.
