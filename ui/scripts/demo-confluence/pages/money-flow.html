The life of a payment at Kettlewell, from the shopper's click to the merchant's bank.

  1. Authorise and capture. Checkout sends the card (or a stored token) to the payments service, which calls the processor. Every capture carries an idempotency key; a retry with the same key must never capture twice (ADR-007).
  2. Settle. Overnight, Settlement batches captured payments per merchant into settlements, cut at the merchant's local midnight (ADO #176 fixes a cut that ran a day early in UTC-5).
  3. Reserve. High-risk merchants keep a rolling reserve: a share of each settlement held back for a fixed number of days (ADR-011, GitHub #18).
  4. Pay out. Settlements roll up into payouts on the merchant's schedule, daily or weekly.
  5. Refund and dispute. Refunds come off the next payout. Disputes (chargebacks) hold the disputed amount until we win or lose them.
  6. Reconcile. At 02:00 UK the reconciliation job checks the ledger against the processor's settlement file.
