TermWhat it means at Kettlewell
CaptureTaking the money from an authorised card payment.
Idempotency keyA client-chosen id sent with each capture, so a retry never captures twice (ADR-007).
SettlementA merchant's captured payments for one day, batched at their local midnight.
PayoutMoney sent to the merchant's bank: one or more settlements, minus refunds, disputes and the reserve.
Rolling reserveA share of each settlement held back for a fixed time for high-risk merchants (ADR-011).
Chargeback / disputeThe cardholder's bank reversing a payment; we hold the amount until it's decided.
Minor unitsThe smallest unit of a currency: pence, cents, or (for JPY and KRW) the yen and won themselves.
TokenWhat a stored card becomes at the edge; only the vault can turn it back into a card (ADR-009).
Change freezeA period when only incident fixes ship. The next one starts 25 November.
