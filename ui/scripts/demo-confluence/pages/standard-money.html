Owner: Priya.

  • Store and send money as integers in minor units (amount_minor), never floats.
  • The number of minor units per major unit depends on the currency. JPY and KRW have zero decimals: ¥500 is 500, not 50000.
  • Format for people with the currency's own exponent. "5.00 JPY" is always a bug (GitHub #38, formatAmount in src/money.js).
  • Round only once, at the edge, and never on a refund: a refund that comes up one unit short is a support ticket waiting to happen (view payments.refund_shortfalls).
