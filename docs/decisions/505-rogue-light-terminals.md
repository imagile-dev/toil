# Two Weeks' Notice keeps its colours on a light terminal (#505)

- **A light scheme derived from a dark one keeps its colours colourful.** Turning a pale tint's lightness over lands next to black (Crate's highlight `#c2e0ff` became `#001e3d`), so on a light terminal the game's headings, items, stairs, staff and morale bar all read as body text, though every one cleared 4.5:1. The colour roles (accent, link, highlight, ok, danger), not ink or muted, are now squeezed into HSL lightness 0.3 to 0.5, keeping their order, and `palette()` still lifts each to the bar. It applies to every theme without a light scheme of its own (Crate, Starship, Sprawl, Overworld), so it changes the welcome menu's light colours for those themes too, not just the game's. Where: `LIGHT_HUE_FLOOR` and `deriveScheme` in `src/welcome/themes/index.ts`.
- **Logged for the maintainer: a derived light scheme is now toil's own invention.** Before, a terminal theme's other scheme was the theme's turned over; now its colours are also held to `LIGHT_HUE_FLOOR`, a light Crate no `theme.json` records and Toil UI doesn't have. The alternative is a light scheme authored in `ui/themes/<name>/theme.json` for Crate, Starship, Sprawl and Overworld and synced here, which is `ui/` work this change stays out of (`test/theme-sync.test.ts` holds each terminal theme to its `theme.json`).
- **256-colour output picks the nearest colour by eye that never loses contrast.** Plain nearest rounding cost several roles their contrast (Overworld's light faint 4.6:1 to 3.8:1; Harvest's and Realm's dark faint to 3.9:1), and macOS Terminal draws 256 colours on white by default. Of the cube and the 24 greys, a colour takes the nearest in OKLab, lightness weighted half and lost chroma counted twice, among those no nearer the background in luminance, or keeping 7.5:1 on it (which leaves 4.5:1 on the common ground of either kind). So a green stays green rather than olive or grey, and a grey stays grey. Where: `to256` in `src/welcome/term.ts`, used by the menu as well as the game. A few dark, low-chroma text colours still come out grey (Harvest's light muted, Frontier's light highlight), because the 256 palette has no colour of their hue dark enough to keep their contrast; accent and danger keep their colour in every theme, and the tests hold that. 16-colour output is left alone: there the terminal's own palette decides the colour.
- **The player is drawn bold.** On a light terminal ink and the walls' muted are both dark, and the `@` sat in the room like one more wall; bold sets it apart on either background. `Cell` gained an optional `bold`, which `renderCells` opens before the colour and closes with the cell. Where: `src/welcome/term.ts`, `src/welcome/rogue/render.ts`.
- **`fg()` says it reads the palette.** At 256 colours it rounds against `caps.palette.bg`, so its parameter now types `palette` as optional; without one it rounds against toil's own dark background, which matches the dark colours a frame with no palette is drawn in.
