# Every theme has a light and a dark scheme (#507)

- **Each new scheme is the theme read the other way round, not a new theme.** The four dark themes gain a daylight edition: Crate as the site's blue on warm drafting paper, Starship's bridge with the lights up, Sprawl's city at dawn, and Overworld's overworld at midday. The three light themes gain a night edition: Harvest by lantern light, Realm's ledger by candlelight, and Frontier by lamplight. Each keeps its hues, type, art and wording. Where: `schemes` in each `ui/themes/<name>/theme.json`.
- **The scheme a theme had stays its default.** It's listed first, so a theme opens as it did whenever the Appearance choice is Light or Dark to match, and the new scheme is added second. Appearance's default, System, follows the operating system, so a light desktop now opens Crate, the default theme, in its light scheme. Before, Crate was dark everywhere. Where: key order in `theme.json`; `tokenCss` in `ui/src/themes.ts` is unchanged.
- **A stylesheet's own colours become theme variables, set per scheme.** Each `theme.css` had colours written for one scheme: glows, washes over the art, panel fills, borders and shadows. Each now declares them once as `--<theme>-*` properties with the old values, so the existing scheme renders as before. The new scheme sets them in `[data-theme][data-scheme="<new>"]`, and again under `prefers-color-scheme` for `system`, the pattern Plain's badge already used. Art keeps its pictures and gets a wash of the new scheme's kind. The exceptions:
  - Crate's art on near-black is inverted and multiplied in light, so the crate keeps its blue without a black box.
  - Sprawl gains `net-map-light.svg`, its net map drawn for a pale ground, because the picture is drawn in the scheme's colours.
- **The terminal stops deriving a scheme.** With both schemes authored and copied, `deriveScheme`, `LIGHT_HUE_FLOOR` and the derived backgrounds are gone, and `TerminalTheme.schemes` requires both. #505 logged `LIGHT_HUE_FLOOR` as toil's own invention for the maintainer; the authored schemes replace it. Where: `src/welcome/themes/index.ts` and `types.ts`.
- **The game's colour test holds the four new light schemes to what #505 asked of the derived ones.** In those four light schemes, highlight, accent and danger stay colours, at least 60 apart in chroma, and stand at least 100 apart from ink. It covers the themes whose light colours used to be derived (Crate, Starship, Sprawl, Overworld). The light schemes Plain, Harvest, Realm and Frontier always had were never held to it, and Plain's highlight, mixed from its accent and ink, is nearer ink than that. Overworld's first light draft failed it (green accent against green ink). Its ink is now a dark brown, `#241c14`, and its accent a deeper leaf green, `#1a7a4a`. Where: `test/rogue.test.ts`.
- **Appearance shows for every theme.** It showed only for a theme with two schemes, which was Plain alone. Where: `ui/src/App.tsx`.
- **Crate's light scheme isn't the site's.** The site is dark only (#442), and `test/brand-sync.test.ts` still holds Crate's dark scheme to it; the light one is Crate's own.
