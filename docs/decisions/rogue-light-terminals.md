# Two Weeks' Notice reads on a light terminal, not only clears the bar there

- **A light scheme derived from a dark one keeps its colours colourful.** Turning a pale tint's lightness over lands next to black (Crate's highlight `#c2e0ff` became `#001e3d`), so on a light terminal the game's headings, items, stairs, staff and morale bar all read as body text, though every one cleared 4.5:1. The colour roles (accent, link, highlight, ok, danger), not ink or muted, are now squeezed into HSL lightness 0.3 to 0.5, keeping their order, and `palette()` still lifts each to the bar. It applies to every theme without a light scheme of its own (Crate, Starship, Sprawl, Overworld), so it changes the welcome menu's light colours for those themes too, not just the game's. Where: `LIGHT_HUE_FLOOR` and `deriveScheme` in `src/welcome/themes/index.ts`. Adding a light scheme to Crate's terminal theme instead was ruled out: `test/theme-sync.test.ts` holds each terminal theme to `ui/themes/<name>/theme.json`, which has only Crate's dark scheme, and this change stays out of `ui/`.
- **256-colour output rounds away from the background, onto the cube or the grey ramp.** Plain nearest rounding cost several roles their contrast (Overworld's light faint 4.6:1 to 3.8:1; Harvest's and Realm's dark faint to 3.9:1), and macOS Terminal draws 256 colours on white by default. Of the cube corners around a colour and the 24 greys, the nearest that is no nearer the background in luminance wins, unless a nearer one still keeps 7.5:1 on it, which leaves 4.5:1 on the common ground of either kind, so a colour well clear of the bar keeps its hue (Crate's dark highlight would otherwise drift to cyan). The greys keep a grey from rounding into a purple or teal corner. Where: `to256` in `src/welcome/term.ts`, used by the menu as well as the game. 16-colour output is left alone: there the terminal's own palette decides the colour.
- **The player is drawn bold.** On a light terminal ink and the walls' muted are both dark, and the `@` sat in the room like one more wall; bold sets it apart on either background. `Cell` gained an optional `bold`, which `renderCells` opens before the colour. Where: `src/welcome/term.ts`, `src/welcome/rogue/render.ts`.
