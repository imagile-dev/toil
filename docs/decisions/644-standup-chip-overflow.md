# Stand-up team chips cut inside their box (#644)

- **The team line's chips are a declared truncation, not a wrap.** They now carry `data-truncate`, like the per-person chips beside them already did, so the title stays one line with an ellipsis and the whole sentence (every fact the chip folds, with its time) stays in the chip's tooltip (`chipTitle`). Wrapping would make one chip two or three lines tall in a row of one-line chips, and the per-person rows already cut the same way. `ui/src/views/Standup.tsx`, `TeamLine`.
- **The sample has a sprint move long enough to overflow at 375px.** The sample worklog adds one yesterday, `moved from Sprint 42 to Sprint 43, by <assignee>`, on the first unfinished item that isn't the scripted blocker. It's marked `observed` (a sync saw it), like the scripted blocker, because the sample items' own history doesn't record it, and the sync test holds the sample's timed facts to what a real sync of the same fixtures records. Without the fix, the layout check fails on checkout-web's stand-up at 375px. `ui/src/fixtures/worklog.ts`.
- **One server test now allows a team line.** Its plain-text stand-up's first line may end with `· Team: …`, which `standupText` already writes when the team has news; the sample's card-payments stand-up now has some. `ui/test/server.test.ts`.
